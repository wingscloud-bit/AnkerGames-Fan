# Game of the Day

Game of the Day is a discovery feature displayed on the AnkerGames homepage.

It highlights a selected game and gives visitors an opportunity to discover something without performing a search.

## Why It Exists

Many users visit a game platform looking for something interesting rather than searching for one specific title.

Game of the Day provides a simple entry point for that type of discovery.

## Homepage Discovery

Because it appears on the homepage, Game of the Day can introduce visitors to titles they may otherwise overlook.

It complements other discovery features such as Trending, Collections, and the Random Game Generator.

## Exploring the Featured Game

Visitors can open the featured game and explore its available information, including the details provided on its game page.

The selected game changes over time, making the homepage an evolving discovery point.
