# Game Discovery Checklist

Use this checklist when looking for a new PC game.

## Before Searching

Ask yourself:

* What genre am I interested in?
* Do I want a newer or older game?
* Do I have a preferred developer?
* Do I want single-player or multiplayer?
* Do I have storage limitations?
* Are there specific gameplay features I want?

## During Discovery

Try:

* Searching for known titles
* Browsing genres
* Filtering by release year
* Exploring developers
* Exploring publishers
* Checking collections
* Looking at trending games
* Using random discovery

## Before Choosing a Game

Review:

* Game description
* Genre
* Release year
* Developer
* Publisher
* Game mode
* System requirements
* Screenshots
* Additional content

## After Finding an Interesting Game

Consider adding it to your personal Watchlist so you can return to it later.

# Game Developers

Game developers play a central role in the PC gaming ecosystem.

Studios range from large organizations with hundreds of employees to independent developers working on individual projects.

## Why Developer Information Matters

Knowing who developed a game can help players discover other titles with similar creative approaches.

A developer's catalog may reveal recurring genres, gameplay systems, artistic styles, or technical approaches.

## Discovering Games Through Developers

A useful discovery process is:

1. Find a game you enjoy.
2. Identify its developer.
3. Explore other games associated with that developer.
4. Compare genres and release periods.
5. Open individual game pages to learn more.

## Independent Developers

Independent developers are an important part of the PC gaming ecosystem.

Smaller teams often experiment with unusual mechanics, art styles, storytelling approaches, or niche genres.

## Developer Information on AnkerGames

AnkerGames provides a dedicated Developers & Publishers area that allows users to explore companies represented in its game library.

This provides an alternative to searching for individual games by title.
